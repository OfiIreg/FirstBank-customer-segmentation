# Public Trust Statement
FirstBank Nigeria Limited — Cross-Sell Propensity Analytics Project

FirstBank uses a predictive model to help decide which customers receive
targeted cross-sell marketing offers. This statement explains, in plain
language, what that means for you as a customer.

**What the model does.** It looks at how you use your account —
how often you transact, how recently, and how much — to estimate how
likely you are to be interested in a relevant product offer. It does not
look at who you are demographically to make that judgment.

**What it does not do.** It does not decide whether you can get a loan,
what you are charged, or whether you can access your account. Those
decisions are made separately, by people, using different processes.

**How we checked it was fair.** Before using this model, we tested
whether it treated different groups of customers — by age and gender —
consistently. We found it did not, initially: older customers and
customers with unspecified gender in our records were being
under-considered. We corrected this and re-tested it. We will keep
re-testing it on an ongoing schedule, not just once at launch.

**Your right to ask.** If you believe a marketing decision affecting you
was made unfairly, you or your relationship manager can request a review
of the specific factors that led to your score, through the Data
Protection Officer's office.

**Where to learn more.** A full technical account of this system,
including its limitations, is available in the project's public Model
Card and Fairness Metrics Report, referenced in this project's GitHub
repository.
